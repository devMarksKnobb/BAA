# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

AIME's community aims to be welcoming, respectful, and constructive for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and collaborative.
- Give feedback about ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Accept constructive criticism gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Maintainer response

Project maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary. Reports should include relevant context and can be sent privately to the repository owner or through GitHub's available reporting tools.

This code of conduct applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project in public.
